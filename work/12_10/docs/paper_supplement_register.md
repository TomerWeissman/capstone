# Supplement: pre-registered hypotheses for the paper (H49-H78)

Every hypothesis tested for this paper, in order, with its pre-registered rule and outcome. Copied from `work/HYPOTHESES.md` on Oct 17, 2026; that file is the source of truth. Each rule was committed to git before its run (see the commit history).

| ID | Hypothesis and rule | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| H49 | How large the 60-minute forecast error is depends more on the patient than on the glucose situation. Group means of absolute error are fitted on validation and scored on test (R^2 of absolute test error). Supported if the patient grouping explains more than the 25-situation grouping on at least 2 of 3 datasets (AZT1D, OhioT1DM, HUPA-UCM). Then repeated on the MetaboNet panel. | Not supported | Patient beats situation on 1 of 3 datasets (HUPA-UCM). R^2 of |test error|: patient 2.8%, -0.8%, 11.2%; situation 5.8%, 3.3%, 8.0%; patient x situation 6.9%, 2.5%, 14.7% (AZT1D, OhioT1DM, HUPA-UCM). OhioT1DM bootstrap interval for patient minus situation excludes zero (-6.6 to -0.6 points). Error size is weakly predictable from any grouping (at most 15%). [verdict](../results/verdict_h49_h50.txt) |
| H50 | Patient-scaled situation bands (absolute errors divided by the patient's validation error scale, then Mondrian conformal by situation, pooled across patients) are calibrated in each patient-by-situation cell better than situation-only Mondrian bands. Supported if the mean distance from 80% over patient-by-situation cells (at least 30 test windows) is at least 20% lower on at least 2 of 3 datasets, with interval score no more than 2% worse. | Not supported | Patient-by-situation error: AZT1D 9.7 to 9.4 (+3%), OhioT1DM 8.3 to 9.0 (-8%), HUPA-UCM 16.7 to 12.1 (+28%, bootstrap +2.6 to +6.9 points). Patient scaling helps only in the heterogeneous cohort. Post hoc: GARCH x situation has the lowest patient-by-situation error on all three (8.5, 7.6, 12.3), also after removing the overall coverage offset. [figure](../figures/h50_patient_scaled_bands.png) |
| H51 | Calibration noise floor: for situation (Mondrian) bands, the remaining per-situation unevenness (dispersion of coverage around the method's overall coverage) is within 1 point of what sampling noise alone produces. Null: situation labels circularly shifted within each patient by at least one day, 50 times, which keeps the autocorrelation of both series. Supported if within 1 point on at least 2 of 3 datasets. | Supported | Situation bands' per-situation unevenness vs noise floor: AZT1D 2.7 vs 2.9, OhioT1DM 4.2 vs 2.5 (excess 1.6), HUPA-UCM 2.1 vs 4.0. Per-situation calibration is at the detectable limit on 2 of 3 datasets. [verdict](../results/verdict_h51_h53.txt) |
| H52 | At the patient-by-situation level, the most even method (GARCH x situation) still exceeds its noise floor (same null) by at least 3 points on at least 2 of 3 datasets: real patient-in-situation miscalibration remains. | Not supported | Patient-by-situation unevenness is mostly sampling noise: floors 6.5 to 15.3 points; real excess of GARCH x situation 1.7, 1.1, 1.2 points; of situation bands 1.2, 1.0, 0.8. GARCH-based methods have lower floors, so their lower raw unevenness (F10) is mostly lower noise, not better calibration. [figure](../figures/h51_noise_floor.png) |
| H53 | Per-patient unevenness of situation bands exceeds its noise floor by at least 2 points on at least 2 of 3 datasets. Null: each patient's coverage varies only by sampling noise, with standard errors from a one-day moving-block bootstrap of that patient's inside/outside sequence; expected dispersion is the mean of sqrt(2/pi) times each standard error. | Supported | Situation bands' per-patient unevenness exceeds its floor by 3.5, 2.3, 9.1 points (AZT1D, OhioT1DM, HUPA-UCM). The real remaining miscalibration is between patients. |
| H54 | External validation of F1 on the MetaboNet panel (8 sources, 20 patients each, never used to design the method): situation (Mondrian) bands have at least 20% lower per-situation calibration error than GARCH in at least 6 of 8 sources. | Confirmed | 8 of 8 external sources: per-situation distance from 80%, situation bands 2.25-4.19 vs GARCH 5.48-6.76 points (Loop, DCLP3, DCLP5, PEDAP, Flair, IOBP2, ReplaceBG, BrisT1D; 153 patients). [verdict](../results/verdict_h54_h56_h60.txt) |
| H55 | External test of F9: on the panel, the situation grouping explains more of the test error size than the patient grouping in at least 5 of 8 sources. | Supported | 7 of 8 sources: situation explains more of |test error| than patient (BrisT1D the exception, 4.8% vs 5.1%). All groupings explain at most 8.8%. [verdict](../results/verdict_h54_h56_h60.txt) |
| H56 | External test of F10: on the panel, GARCH x situation has the lowest patient-by-situation dispersion of the 7 band methods in at least 5 of 8 sources. | Supported | 7 of 8 sources (Flair the exception). Caveat from H52/H72: mostly lower noise floor (more even daily coverage), not better conditional calibration. [verdict](../results/verdict_h54_h56_h60.txt) |
| H57 | Mechanism for F10: GARCH-scaled bands spread their misses out in time. Mean length of runs of consecutive misses (test windows, per patient, pooled) is at least 15% shorter for GARCH x situation than for situation bands on at least 2 of 3 datasets. | Not supported | Mean miss-run length GARCH x situation vs situation bands: 4.63 vs 5.13, 5.03 vs 5.20, 5.29 vs 5.98 windows (1 to 12% shorter; rule 15%). The lower noise floors of GARCH-based bands are not explained by shorter miss streaks. [verdict](../results/verdict_h57_h59.txt) |
| H58 | Adaptive conformal inference (Gibbs and Candes 2021) on top of situation bands, run per patient over the test period with feedback delayed 60 minutes (gamma 0.005; 0.001 and 0.02 reported as sensitivity), brings per-patient unevenness to within 1 point of its noise floor on at least 2 of 3 datasets, keeps per-situation excess at or below 1.5 points, and has an interval score no more than 3% worse than situation bands. | Not supported | ACI (gamma 0.005) removes per-patient excess on all three (-0.87, -0.84, -0.38 points) and improves interval score (113.0 vs 113.5; 130.5 vs 130.8; 107.8 vs 110.8), but per-situation excess rises to 2.93 (OhioT1DM) and 1.75 (HUPA-UCM), above the 1.5 rule. Gamma 0.001 meets all criteria on AZT1D only. Gamma 0.02 too jumpy (wider, worse interval score). [figure](../figures/h57_h59_aci.png) |
| H59 | Second try at H58 (recorded as such): adapt a per-patient multiplicative scale instead of the quantile level. Half-width = situation quantile (80%) x exp(theta_t), theta updated by eta x (miss 60 minutes earlier - 0.2), eta 0.01 (0.003 and 0.03 as sensitivity). Same three criteria as H58 on at least 2 of 3 datasets. | Supported (2 of 3) | eta 0.01: per-patient excess -0.88, -0.91, +0.43 points; per-situation excess 0.93, 2.52 (fails, OhioT1DM), 1.18; interval score 112.0 vs 113.5, 130.2 vs 130.8, 107.5 vs 110.8 (situation bands). Across all adaptive variants, faster adaptation trades per-situation evenness for per-patient evenness. Second try after H58. [verdict](../results/verdict_h57_h59.txt) |
| H60 | External test of H59 on the MetaboNet panel: the per-patient scale-adaptive situation band (eta 0.01) meets the three H58 criteria (per-patient excess <= 1 point, per-situation excess <= 1.5 points, interval score <= 3% worse than situation bands) in at least 5 of 8 sources. | Not supported | 2 of 8 sources meet all criteria. The per-patient fix replicates in all 8 (per-patient excess -0.55 to -0.84 points; interval score better or within 0.5%), but per-situation excess is 1.27-2.27 (over 1.5 in 6 of 8): the same trade-off as internally. [verdict](../results/verdict_h54_h56_h60.txt) |
| H61 | A gradient-boosting model of the absolute 60-minute error, trained on validation windows (all patients) with rich context (level, 30-min and 2-h trend, acceleration, carbs and bolus in 2 h, hour, GARCH scale, patient error scale, most recent known errors), explains at least twice the variance of absolute test error that the patient x situation group means do (H49), on at least 2 of 3 datasets. | Not supported | R^2 of |test error|: gradient boosting 9.8%, 3.7%, 19.9% vs patient x situation 6.9%, 2.5%, 14.7% (about 1.4x; rule 2x). Error size stays mostly unpredictable even with rich context. [verdict](../results/verdict_h61_h64.txt) |
| H62 | The most important single feature of that model (permutation importance on test) is the most recently observed forecast error, on at least 2 of 3 datasets. | Not supported | Top features: forecast value (AZT1D, OhioT1DM) and patient error scale (HUPA-UCM). The latest known error ranks low (permutation drop under 1 point on 2 of 3). Errors barely cluster at the 60-minute horizon, which is why GARCH's scale does not predict error size. [figure](../figures/h61_h62_ml_error_size.png) |
| H63 | A conformalized quantile regression band built on the same rich features has an interval score at least 3% better than the per-patient scale-adaptive situation band (H59) on at least 2 of 3 datasets, with per-situation and per-patient excess both at or below 1.5 points. | Not supported | CQR with rich features is the sharpest (interval score 109.0 vs 112.0; 129.0 vs 130.1; 100.6 vs 107.5 for the H59 band) but miscalibrated: situation excess 3.95, 2.36, 2.91; patient excess 2.82, 2.29, 6.17 points. |
| H64 | Third design for a band that is both sharp and conditionally calibrated (recorded as such): rich-feature CQR whose conformal correction is computed separately in each situation (Mondrian CQR), times the per-patient online scale (eta 0.01). Supported if, on at least 2 of 3 datasets, its interval score is at least 2% better than the H59 band and both excesses are at or below 1.5 points. | Not supported | Best interval score of all methods on all three datasets (108.0, 128.1, 99.6 vs 112.0, 130.1, 107.5 for the H59 band; 3.5%, 1.5%, 7.3% better) and per-patient excess at or below zero, but per-situation excess 2.15, 2.07, 2.29 (rule 1.5). Third design; tuning stopped per protocol. [verdict](../results/verdict_h61_h64.txt) |
| H65 | Across all patients with forecasters (AZT1D, OhioT1DM, HUPA-UCM, MetaboNet panel; about 220), the log of a patient's test RMSE is explained mostly by the log of their glucose SD (computed on the training period): leave-one-source-out R^2 at least 0.60. | Not supported | Leave-one-source-out R^2 0.44 (rule 0.60), slope 0.64 on log-log; 215 patients, 11 sources. Glucose variability explains less than half of between-patient forecast difficulty. [verdict](../results/verdict_h65_h67.txt) [figure](../figures/h65_patient_difficulty.png) |
| H66 | After adjusting for glucose SD, delivery modality matters: closed-loop (AID) patients' RMSE differs from non-AID patients' by at least 5%, with a 90% source-cluster bootstrap interval excluding zero. Direction not predicted. | Not supported | AID vs non-AID after SD: +2.4% (90% source-cluster bootstrap -4.3% to +8.9%). [verdict](../results/verdict_h65_h67.txt) |
| H67 | After adjusting for glucose SD, children (under 18) have RMSE at least 5% different from adults, interval excluding zero. Direction not predicted. | Supported | Children vs adults after SD: +7.5% RMSE (90% source-cluster bootstrap +3.3% to +12.4%); 69 children. [verdict](../results/verdict_h65_h67.txt) |
| H68 | Safety non-inferiority: the situation x patient-scale band (H59) catches no fewer Clarke zone D points than situation bands (no more than 2 points lower) on at least 2 of 3 datasets. GARCH reported for reference. | Supported | Zone D caught, situation x patient scale vs situation bands: AZT1D 29.4% vs 22.4%, OhioT1DM 44.2% vs 41.4%, HUPA-UCM 51.7% vs 45.8%. GARCH still catches most (39.1%, 62.1%, 68.9%). [verdict](../results/verdict_h68.txt) |
| H69 | The Round 2-3 findings do not depend on the forecaster: with the CNN-Transformer (cnn_transformer_v0) on AZT1D and OhioT1DM, (a) situation bands' per-situation unevenness is within 1.5 points of its noise floor on both, and (b) the per-patient scale-adaptive band cuts per-patient excess above the floor by at least half on both, with interval score no worse than situation bands by more than 3%. | Supported | CNN-Transformer: situation bands' per-situation excess 0.61 (AZT1D), 1.38 (OhioT1DM); patient-scale band per-patient excess 3.50 to -0.84 and 2.61 to -0.87; interval score 116.4 vs 118.3 and 134.5 vs 134.9. [verdict](../results/verdict_h69.txt) |
| H70 | Cold-start calibration: when a patient's band is calibrated only on other patients' validation data (leave-one-patient-out situation quantiles), the online per-patient scale (eta 0.01) cuts per-patient excess above the noise floor by at least half compared with no scaling, and lands within 1 point of the own-data patient-scale band, on at least 2 of 3 datasets. The forecaster itself is still per-patient (only the band is cold). | Supported | Per-patient excess, cold situation bands vs cold + patient scale: 3.59 to -0.87 (AZT1D), 2.47 to -0.89 (OhioT1DM), 11.45 to 0.49 (HUPA-UCM); own-data + patient scale -0.88, -0.91, 0.43. Interval scores within 0.3 of own-data. [verdict](../results/verdict_h70.txt) |
| H71 | The structure holds at a 30-minute horizon. Plain CNN-LSTMs retrained for 30 minutes on AZT1D and OhioT1DM (one seed per patient): (a) situation bands' per-situation excess above the noise floor is at or below 1.5 points on both; (b) the online per-patient scale (feedback delayed 30 minutes) cuts per-patient excess by at least half on both, with interval score no more than 3% worse than situation bands. | Not supported (narrowly) | 30 minutes (RMSE 21.6 AZT1D, 23.2 OhioT1DM): (a) situation bands' per-situation excess 1.56 (AZT1D, rule 1.5) and 1.25; (b) holds: per-patient excess 3.71 to -0.45 and 1.83 to -0.85, interval score 73.9 vs 75.8 and 80.1 vs 81.0. Situation explains more error size than patient at 30 minutes too (R^2 7.6% vs 1.7%; 4.3% vs -2.3%). [verdict](../results/verdict_h71.txt) |
| H72 | Mechanism for the lower noise floors of GARCH-scaled bands (open after H57): they even out coverage across days. The SD of daily coverage (per patient-day with at least 200 test windows, pooled) is at least 15% lower for GARCH x situation than for situation bands on at least 2 of 3 datasets. | Supported | SD of daily coverage, GARCH x situation vs situation bands: 7.2 vs 10.3 (AZT1D), 6.3 vs 9.3 (OhioT1DM), 8.9 vs 12.4 (HUPA-UCM). The per-patient scale-adaptive band is the most even: 5.5, 5.9, 6.7. Explains the lower noise floors of GARCH-scaled bands (H52, H57). [verdict](../results/verdict_h72.txt) |
| H73 | Fourth design for the patient-level fix (recorded as such), motivated by paper Figure 1 (a single patient scale over-covers rare extreme situations): one online scale per patient per glucose level band (5 per patient), each updated only by misses in its own level (eta 0.01, feedback 60 minutes later). Same criteria as H58 (per-patient excess <= 1, per-situation excess <= 1.5, interval score <= 3% worse than situation bands) on at least 2 of 3 datasets, and per-situation excess lower than the single-scale band (H59) on at least 2 of 3. | Supported (2 of 3) | Per-level patient scale: per-situation excess 0.75, 1.15, 0.17 (single scale 0.93, 2.52, 1.18); per-patient excess -0.21, -0.42, 4.72 (fails on HUPA-UCM, whose test periods are about 2.6 days); interval score 111.8, 130.0, 107.9. Daily-coverage SD 6.8, 7.7, 9.0 (single scale 5.5, 5.9, 6.7). Fourth design; tuning stopped. [verdict](../results/verdict_h73.txt) |
| H74 | External test of H73 on the MetaboNet panel (45-day records): the per-patient per-level online scale meets the H58 criteria (per-patient excess <= 1, per-situation excess <= 1.5, interval score <= 3% worse than situation bands) in at least 5 of 8 sources. Per-patient excess -0.30 to 0.52; per-situation excess 0.61-1.62 vs 1.27-2.27 for the single scale; best interval score in 6 of 8. [verdict](../results/verdict_h74.txt) |
| H75 | Seed robustness: with every AZT1D and OhioT1DM forecaster retrained with a second seed (one new seed per patient), (a) situation bands' per-situation excess is at or below 1.5 points and (b) the per-patient scale cuts per-patient excess by at least half with interval score no more than 3% worse, on both datasets. | Not supported (part a) | Second seed: (a) situation excess -0.45 (AZT1D) but 1.87 (OhioT1DM; seed 1 gave 1.62, so OhioT1DM keeps a small real situation residual); (b) holds: per-patient excess 4.52 to -0.83 and 2.90 to -0.95, interval score 114.8 vs 117.6 and 130.9 vs 132.2.
