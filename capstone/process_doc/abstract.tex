This document describes how I carried out the first two months of my capstone on glucose forecasting for type 1 diabetes, from early September to late October 2026. It follows the project from a replication of the GLIMMER study, through a seed bug that erased its main gain and a set of uncertainty bands that raised too many false alarms, to the question I study now: whether the band around a forecast stays honest in each glucose situation, for each patient and on each day. It explains how I defined success before every test, using a written research protocol and a hypothesis register that keeps every failure, and how I checked results on eight outside studies never used to design the methods. It also covers my planning and how it compared with what happened, my use of an AI coding assistant and the checks I placed on it, the feedback I received from my advisor and second reader, and the work that remains before the Full Draft in December.
